# Dental Cleaning (Scaling & Polishing) Post-Procedure Aftercare Guide

**Document ID:** `DENT-034` | **Version:** `1.2` | **Status:** `active` | **Effective Date:** `2026-01-01`  
**Clinical Authority:** General Dentistry Lead (Dr. Kavita Patwardhan, BDS, Reg. No. `MH-DCI-14821`, `STF-005`)  
**Applicable Services:** Routine Ultrasonic Scaling & Polishing (`SRV-003`) and Deep Scaling & Root Planing (`SRV-003A`)

---

## 1. Immediate Post-Cleaning Care (First 24 Hours)

Professional ultrasonic scaling and polishing (`DENT-012`) removes mineralized calculus (tartar), bacterial biofilm, and extrinsic surface stains from above and below the gumline in Operatory 4 (`OP-04`). Immediately after the procedure:

1. **Wait 30 Minutes Before Eating or Drinking (If Fluoride Rinse Applied):** If your clinician applied a neutral sodium fluoride desensitizing rinse at the conclusion of `SRV-003` or `SRV-003A`, avoid eating, drinking, or rinsing with water for **30 minutes** so fluoride ions can absorb into the enamel surface.
2. **Local Anaesthesia Caution (`SRV-003A` Only):** If you underwent deep quadrant scaling and root planing (`SRV-003A`) with local anaesthesia, avoid chewing solid foods or drinking hot beverages for **2 to 4 hours** until lip, cheek, and tongue numbness wears off completely.
3. **24-Hour Stain & Acid Avoidance Window:** Newly polished enamel surfaces are temporarily free of the protective salivary pellicle layer for the first 24 hours. Avoid dark chromogenic foods and beverages—including **tea, black coffee, red wine, turmeric-heavy curries, beetroot, soy sauce, and tobacco/paan**—as well as highly acidic citrus juices or carbonated sodas during the first **24 hours**.

---

## 2. Normal Post-Procedure Sensations & Recovery Timeline

Removing thick calculus deposits exposes root surfaces and gingival margins that were previously insulated by tartar. Mild, temporary symptoms are completely normal and indicate healthy tissue healing:

| Recovery Window | Normal Expected Experience | Recommended Home Care |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First 24 Hours** | Slight pinkish tinge when spitting during the first toothbrushing; mild gum tenderness along inflamed margins | Rinse gently 2–3 times with lukewarm salt water (½ teaspoon salt in 250 ml warm water) to soothe gingival tissues |
| **Days 1 to 5** | Mild, brief cold sensitivity to chilled water or air, especially after deep root planing (`SRV-003A`) | Use a soft-bristled toothbrush and a potassium-nitrate desensitizing toothpaste twice daily; avoid ice-cold drinks |
| **Days 5 to 14** | Gums tighten and return to a firm, pale-pink, non-bleeding state; thermal sensitivity resolves | Maintain regular twice-daily brushing and daily interdental cleaning |

*Clinical Clarification: Ultrasonic piezo scaling uses high-frequency micro-vibrations and sterile water irrigation to dislodge tartar—it does **not** scratch, thin, or weaken tooth enamel. Slight gaps noticed between lower front teeth after cleaning simply reflect the removal of hardened tartar bridges that previously filled those spaces.*

---

## 3. Daily Oral Hygiene Maintenance Protocol

To prevent rapid plaque remineralization and preserve gingival health between clinic visits:

* **Toothbrushing Technique:** Brush twice daily for 2 full minutes using a **soft-bristled manual or electric toothbrush** angled at 45 degrees toward the gumline with gentle circular strokes. Avoid hard-bristled brushes or aggressive horizontal scrubbing, which can cause gum recession.
* **Interdental Cleaning:** Clean between teeth once every evening using waxed dental floss, a water flosser, or appropriately sized interdental brushes recommended during your hygiene appointment.
* **Recommended Recall Schedule:**
  * Patients completing routine `SRV-003` with healthy gums should schedule maintenance scaling every **6 months** (eligible under Annual Preventive Oral Wellness Plan `PKG-001` in `DENT-023`).
  * Patients completing deep periodontal root planing (`SRV-003A`) should return every **3 to 4 months** for periodontal maintenance reviews.

---

## 4. When to Contact the Clinic

Mild gum soreness and cold sensitivity should steadily improve over 3 to 5 days. Contact BrightSmile Dental Care reception at **`+91-20-5550-0140`** (Monday – Saturday, 09:30 AM – 08:30 PM IST) if:

* Gum bleeding persists spontaneously beyond **48 hours** after cleaning.
* Localized throbbing pain, gum swelling, or pus discharge develops around a specific tooth (which may indicate an underlying periodontal abscess requiring clinical review).
* Severe cold sensitivity fails to improve after **14 days** of desensitizing toothpaste use.

*(For urgent after-hours post-procedure guidance, call `+91-20-5550-0199`; for red-flag systemic symptoms such as spreading facial swelling or fever > 101°F, follow `DENT-035` immediately.)*
