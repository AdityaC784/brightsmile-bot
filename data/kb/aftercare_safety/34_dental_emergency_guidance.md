# Dental Emergency Triage & Medical Red-Flag Escalation Protocol (`ESC-01` to `ESC-04`)

## 1. Purpose & Outpatient Facility Boundary

BrightSmile Dental Care is a private multi-specialty outpatient dental clinic located on the 2nd Floor of Aura Business Plaza, Baner Road, Pune (`DENT-001`). While our clinical team maintains dedicated daily emergency triage windows (**12:30 PM – 01:30 PM** and **07:30 PM – 08:30 PM**, Monday through Saturday; `DENT-002`) and immediate walk-in trauma handling (`WLK-T1` in `DENT-011`), BrightSmile Dental Care **is not an inpatient hospital** and does not operate overnight surgical beds or intensive care units.

This protocol defines four standardized triage tiers (**`ESC-01` through `ESC-04`**) so patients, front-desk staff, and the AI customer-support assistant route life-threatening medical emergencies to hospital emergency departments without delay while providing safe, conservative first-aid instructions for urgent dental events.

## 3. Time-Critical First-Aid Protocol for Knocked-Out Permanent Tooth (`ESC-03`)

If an adult (permanent) tooth is completely knocked out of its socket, survival of the periodontal ligament cells on the root surface depends on reaching a dentist within **30 to 60 minutes**:

1. **Pick Up by the Crown Only:** Hold the tooth strictly by the white chewing crown. **Never touch, scrub, scrape, or wipe the root surface.**
2. **Gentle Rinse If Dirty:** If the tooth fell onto the ground, rinse it gently for no more than **10 seconds** with cold milk or sterile normal saline. Do not use tap water, soap, alcohol, or tissue paper.
3. **Reinsert Gently (If Alert & Adult) OR Store in Cold Milk:**
   * If the patient is conscious, calm, and an adult, gently place the tooth back into the socket facing the right direction and bite softly on a clean handkerchief.
   * If reinsertion is not possible (or for children), immediately submerge the tooth in a clean cup of **cold pasteurized milk** or sterile saline (or inside the patient's cheek pouch if alert and old enough not to swallow it). **Never store the tooth dry or in plain tap water.**
4. **Immediate Clinic Arrival:** Call **`+91-20-5550-0140`** while en route to Aura Business Plaza, Baner Road (`DENT-003`) for **`WLK-T1`** trauma stabilization. *(Note: Knocked-out baby/primary teeth in young children must **not** be reinserted, as reinsertion can damage the developing permanent tooth bud underneath.)*

## 4. Strict Prohibition on Remote Prescriptions & Self-Medication

* **No Remote Antibiotic or Analgesic Prescriptions:** BrightSmile Dental Care clinicians, receptionists, and the AI assistant **never** prescribe antibiotics, opioid painkillers, or specific medication dosages over chat, email, or telephone without an in-clinic clinical examination (`POL-ESC-01`, `DENT-036`).
* **Aspirin Warning for Oral Bleeding:** Patients experiencing active dental or post-extraction bleeding must **never** place an aspirin tablet directly against the gums (which causes severe chemical mucosal burns) and should avoid non-prescribed blood-thinning analgesics.
