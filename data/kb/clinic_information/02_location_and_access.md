# Clinic Location, Directions, Parking, and Accessibility Guide

## 1. Official Clinic Address and Geographic Context

BrightSmile Dental Care is located in the Baner–Balewadi commercial corridor in north-west Pune, Maharashtra, India. The clinic occupies Suite 201–204 on the 2nd Floor of Aura Business Plaza along main Baner Road, offering convenient road connectivity from Baner, Balewadi, Aundh, Wakad, Pashan, Sus, and Hinjawadi Phase 1.

| Location Attribute | Verified Clinic Detail |

| **Clinic Name** | BrightSmile Dental Care |
| **Building & Floor** | 2nd Floor, Aura Business Plaza |
| **Street & Locality** | Baner Road, Near Balewadi High Street Junction |
| **City, State & PIN Code** | Pune, Maharashtra 411045, India |

Patients arriving for their first visit are advised to reach Aura Business Plaza at least **15 minutes prior** to their scheduled appointment time to complete new-patient identity verification and medical history registration (`DENT-025`, `POL-REG-01`).

## 2. Road Directions by Approach Corridor

### 2.1 Coming from Aundh / University Circle (via Baner Road)
1. Travel north-west along main Baner Road past Kapil Malhar Chowk and Pan Card Club Road junction.
2. Continue straight toward Balewadi High Street Junction.
3. Approximately 350 metres before reaching Balewadi Phata Bus Stop, Aura Business Plaza is situated on the left-hand side of Baner Road.
4. Enter via the main commercial vehicle gate on Baner Road and follow directional signage to Basement Level 1 parking or the ground-floor lobby.

### 2.2 Coming from Wakad / Hinjawadi / Mumbai–Bengaluru Highway (NH-48)
1. Exit NH-48 at the Balewadi Stadium / Baner–Balewadi interchange toward Balewadi High Street.
2. Merge onto Baner Road heading south-east toward Aundh.
3. Cross Balewadi Phata Bus Stop; Aura Business Plaza is located 350 metres ahead on the right-hand side.
4. Use the designated U-turn median break 120 metres past the building to enter the Aura Business Plaza driveway safely.

### 2.3 Coming from Pashan / Sus Road
1. Proceed north along Pashan–Sus Road and turn right onto the Baner–Pashan Link Road.
2. At the Baner Road T-junction, turn left toward Balewadi High Street Junction.
3. Aura Business Plaza is 600 metres ahead on the left-hand side, 350 metres before Balewadi Phata Bus Stop.

## 3. Public Transit, Cab, and Auto-Rickshaw Access

* **PMPML Public Bus Service:** Alight at **Balewadi Phata Bus Stop** on Baner Road (served by routes connecting Pune Station, Shivajinagar, Swargate, Aundh, and Hinjawadi). Walk 350 metres south-east along the paved pedestrian footpath to Aura Business Plaza.
* **App-Based Cabs (Ola / Uber) & Auto-Rickshaws:** Set the drop-off pin to **Aura Business Plaza, Baner Road, Pune 411045**. Cabs and auto-rickshaws may pull into the covered ground-floor drop-off bay directly outside the main reception lobby for safe, step-free passenger alighting.

## 4. Patient Parking Facilities

BrightSmile Dental Care maintains dedicated, complimentary patient parking bays inside Aura Business Plaza for the duration of active dental appointments:

| Vehicle Type | Allocated Capacity | Exact Location & Bay Numbers | Access Procedure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Two-Wheelers (Scooters / Motorcycles)** | 6 dedicated bays | Ground-Level Covered Visitor Zone, Bays `TW-01` to `TW-06` | Inform security at main gate of BrightSmile Dental Care appointment |
| **Four-Wheelers (Cars / SUVs)** | 3 dedicated slots | Basement Level 1 (`B1`), Bays `B1-14`, `B1-15`, and `B1-16` | Collect visitor token at ramp boom barrier; validate at clinic reception |

### Parking Conditions and Overflow Guidance
* Patient parking bays (`TW-01`–`TW-06` and `B1-14`–`B1-16`) are reserved strictly for patients actively attending appointments at BrightSmile Dental Care and cannot be used for full-day commercial parking.
* During peak Saturday morning hours (10:30 AM – 01:00 PM), if all three dedicated four-wheeler slots (`B1-14` to `B1-16`) are occupied, patients may use paid municipal street parking along the Baner Road service lane or the Balewadi High Street multi-level visitor parking complex located 400 metres away.
* BrightSmile Dental Care is not responsible for vehicles parked in unauthorized fire lanes or neighbouring commercial bays.

## 5. Step-Free Wheelchair and Senior Accessibility

Aura Business Plaza and BrightSmile Dental Care are designed to accommodate senior citizens, patients with mobility aids, wheelchair users, and parents with strollers:

1. **Step-Free Entrance:** A gentle-gradient paved ramp connects the ground-floor cab drop-off bay and Basement Level 1 parking lobby to the main elevator bank without steps.
2. **Wheelchair-Accessible Elevator (`Lift B`):** Take **Lift B** (wide-door stretcher/wheelchair-compatible passenger elevator located on the right side of the ground lobby) directly to the **2nd Floor**. Lift B features tactile Braille floor buttons and audio floor announcements.
3. **Clinic Interior Layout:** The main reception corridor, patient waiting lounge, accessible washroom, digital OPG room (`EQP-006`), and all four dental operatories (`OP-01` through `OP-04`) feature wide doorways (minimum 1,000 mm clear width) and flush, threshold-free flooring.
4. **In-Clinic Mobility Assistance:** Patients requiring a clinic transfer wheelchair from the ground-floor drop-off bay or Basement Level 1 (`B1-14`–`B1-16`) may call `+91-20-5550-0140` 10 minutes before arrival so a patient care coordinator can meet them at the lobby with the clinic wheelchair.

## 6. Nearby Medical Emergency Facilities

While BrightSmile Dental Care handles urgent outpatient dental conditions during operating hours (`DENT-002`, `DENT-011`), patients presenting with life-threatening or systemic airway/facial emergencies (`DENT-035`, `ESC-01`) require immediate hospital emergency care. The nearest tertiary hospitals with 24/7 emergency departments are:

* **Jupiter Hospital, Baner** — Approximately 1.4 km (5–7 minutes by road via Baner Road).
* **Manipal Hospital, Baner** — Approximately 2.1 km (8–10 minutes by road via Baner–Balewadi link).
