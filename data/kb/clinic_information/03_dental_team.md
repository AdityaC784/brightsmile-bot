# BrightSmile Dental Care — Clinical & Administrative Team Directory

## 1. Overview of Clinical Governance and Staffing

BrightSmile Dental Care delivers multi-specialty outpatient dental care through a permanent team of five registered dental practitioners and one full-time healthcare operations administrator. Every clinician practising at BrightSmile Dental Care holds a recognized Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) or Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) degree and maintains active registration with the Maharashtra State Dental Council / Dental Council of India (DCI).

Clinical protocols, infection-control audits, and complex multidisciplinary treatment plans are overseen by Founder and Chief Endodontist **Dr. Aarav Deshmukh (`STF-001`)**, while patient scheduling, insurance reimbursement documentation, billing compliance, and grievance resolution are managed by Clinic Operations and Billing Manager **Vikram Gokhale (`STF-006`)**.

## 3. Detailed Clinician and Leadership Profiles

### 3.1 Dr. Aarav Deshmukh (`STF-001`) — Founder & Chief Endodontist
* **Academic Qualifications:** BDS, MDS (Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics)
* **Registration Number:** `MH-DCI-10482`
* **Regular Clinic Days:** Monday through Saturday (typical chairside window 10:00 AM – 01:30 PM and 03:00 PM – 08:00 PM)
* **Primary Operatory:** Operatory 1 (`OP-01` — Endodontic & Micro-restorative Suite)
* **Clinical Scope & Responsibilities:**
  * Performs anterior, premolar (`SRV-005`), and complex multi-canal molar root canal treatments (`SRV-005A`) utilizing a Carl Zeiss dental operating microscope, electronic apex locator, and rotary endomotor systems (`DENT-014`).
  * Manages deep multi-surface composite restorations (`SRV-004A`), cracked-tooth diagnostics, and endodontic retreatments.
  * Serves as the clinical authority for informed consent standards (`DENT-028`), clinical aftercare protocols (`DENT-031`), and Level 2 clinical quality escalations (`DENT-030`, `POL-CMP-01`).

### 3.2 Dr. Meera Kulkarni (`STF-002`) — Senior Consultant Orthodontist
* **Academic Qualifications:** BDS, MDS (Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics)
* **Registration Number:** `MH-DCI-11934`
* **Regular Clinic Days:** Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday (typical chairside window 11:00 AM – 01:30 PM and 03:30 PM – 08:00 PM)
* **Primary Operatory:** Operatory 3 (`OP-03` — Orthodontics & General Dentistry Suite)
* **Clinical Scope & Responsibilities:**
  * Conducts comprehensive orthodontic evaluations, digital 3D intraoral optical scans using the iTero scanner (`EQP-003`), and cephalometric/OPG treatment planning (`DENT-017`).
  * Administers full-arch conventional metal braces (`SRV-008`) and comprehensive clear aligner packages (`SRV-008A`, up to 24 trays).
  * Manages adolescent and adult malocclusion correction, space maintenance, and post-orthodontic retainer protocols.

### 3.3 Dr. Rohan Joshi (`STF-003`) — Consultant Oral Surgeon
* **Academic Qualifications:** BDS, MDS (Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery)
* **Registration Number:** `MH-DCI-12710`
* **Regular Clinic Days:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday (typical chairside window 10:30 AM – 01:30 PM and 04:00 PM – 08:00 PM)
* **Primary Operatory:** Operatory 2 (`OP-02` — Oral Surgery & Implant Suite)
* **Clinical Scope & Responsibilities:**
  * Performs surgical extractions of impacted wisdom teeth (`SRV-007A`) and complex multi-rooted extractions (`DENT-016`) using ultrasonic piezotome bone-surgery instrumentation (`EQP-002`).
  * Oversees pre-surgical vital-sign monitoring, external CBCT diagnostic referrals for mandibular nerve proximity assessment, and post-surgical haemostasis protocols (`DENT-032`).
  * Evaluates acute maxillofacial infections and directs severe airway/facial swelling cases to tertiary hospital emergency care (`DENT-035`, `ESC-01`).

### 3.4 Dr. Sneha Nair (`STF-004`) — Consultant Paediatric Dentist
* **Academic Qualifications:** BDS, MDS (Paediatric & Preventive Dentistry)
* **Registration Number:** `MH-DCI-13509`
* **Regular Clinic Days:**
  * Monday, Wednesday, and Friday: 09:30 AM – 01:30 PM and 04:00 PM – 07:30 PM
  * Saturday: **Morning Session only (09:30 AM – 01:30 PM)**
* **Primary Operatory:** Operatory 4 (`OP-04` — Paediatric & Preventive Hygiene Suite)
* **Clinical Scope & Responsibilities:**
  * Conducts dedicated paediatric dental consultations for children under 14 years of age (`SRV-001A`) and administers the Kids Cavity Shield & Hygiene Package (`PKG-003`).
  * Delivers preventive topical fluoride therapy, pit and fissure sealants, behaviour-guided paediatric composite fillings, and primary-tooth pulp care.
  * Authors and enforces BrightSmile's minor-patient accompaniment and guardian consent governance (`DENT-029`, `POL-PED-01`).

### 3.5 Dr. Kavita Patwardhan (`STF-005`) — Associate General Dentist
* **Academic Qualifications:** BDS
* **Registration Number:** `MH-DCI-14821`
* **Regular Clinic Days:** Monday through Saturday (09:30 AM – 01:30 PM and 02:30 PM – 08:30 PM)
* **Primary Operatories:** Operatory 3 (`OP-03`) and Operatory 4 (`OP-04`)
* **Clinical Scope & Responsibilities:**
  * Conducts comprehensive adult oral examinations (`SRV-001`), routine ultrasonic scaling and polishing (`SRV-003`), deep quadrant scaling and root planing (`SRV-003A`, `DENT-012`), and single-surface composite fillings (`SRV-004`, `DENT-013`).
  * Performs simple non-surgical tooth extractions (`SRV-007`), prepares and fits Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (`SRV-006`) and monolithic zirconia dental crowns (`SRV-006A`, `DENT-015`), and administers in-office LED power whitening (`SRV-009`) and custom take-home whitening trays (`SRV-009A`, `DENT-018`).
  * Leads daytime emergency triage assessments (`WLK-T1` and `WLK-T2`) alongside Dr. Aarav Deshmukh.

### 3.6 Vikram Gokhale (`STF-006`) — Clinic Operations & Billing Manager
* **Academic Qualifications:** BHA (Bachelor of Hospital Administration)
* **Role Classification:** Non-clinical Administrative & Compliance Lead (does not diagnose or perform dental procedures)
* **Regular Working Days:** Monday through Saturday (09:30 AM – 08:30 PM)
* **Operational Responsibilities:**
  * Manages front-desk scheduling, slot-hold deposit accounting (`POL-BKG-02`), cancellation/rescheduling fee waivers (`DENT-007`, `DENT-008`), and billing transparency (`DENT-019`, `DENT-021`, `DENT-024`).
  * Prepares and certifies insurance reimbursement document packs (`DENT-022`), coordinates corporate OPD wallet authorizations (MediBuddy and Vidal Health), and serves as the clinic's Data Privacy Officer under the DPDP Act, 2023 (`DENT-027`).
  * Handles Level 1 patient complaints and service-quality feedback (`DENT-030`, `POL-CMP-01`).
