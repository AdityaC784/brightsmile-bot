# Simple & Surgical Tooth Extraction Guide (`SRV-007` & `SRV-007A`)

## 1. Preservation-First Philosophy & Extraction Indications

At BrightSmile Dental Care, natural tooth preservation is always our primary objective (`DENT-001`). Extraction is recommended only when a tooth cannot be predictably restored through fillings (`DENT-013`), root canal therapy (`DENT-014`), or periodontal care (`DENT-012`), or when an impacted third molar (wisdom tooth) threatens adjacent teeth or causes recurrent pericoronitis.

Simple extractions (`SRV-007`) are performed by Associate General Dentist **Dr. Kavita Patwardhan (`STF-005`)** or Consultant Oral Surgeon **Dr. Rohan Joshi (`STF-003`)**, whereas surgical and impacted wisdom tooth extractions (`SRV-007A`) are performed exclusively by **Dr. Rohan Joshi (`STF-003`, BDS, MDS Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery, Reg. `MH-DCI-12710`)** on **Monday, Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday** in Operatory 2 (`OP-02`).

## 2. Comparison of Extraction Categories: `SRV-007` vs. `SRV-007A`

| Clinical & Operational Parameter | `SRV-007`: Simple Non-Surgical Tooth Extraction | `SRV-007A`: Surgical Impacted Wisdom Tooth Extraction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clinical Presentation** | Fully erupted tooth with intact crown and straightforward root anatomy (e.g., non-restorable decayed tooth, grade-III mobile periodontal tooth, retained primary tooth) | Partially erupted or fully bone/gum-impacted mandibular or maxillary third molar, fractured root tips below gumline, or curved/divergent roots |
| **Surgical Technique** | Atraumatic luxation with periotomes/elevators and extraction forceps; no surgical flap or bone removal required | Minor mucoperiosteal flap reflection, conservative ultrasonic bone troughing via **Acteon Piezotome (`EQP-010`)**, tooth sectioning if needed, and resorbable/non-resorbable sutures |
| **Required Pre-Op Imaging** | Single-tooth digital RVG X-ray (`SRV-002`, ₹350) | Full-mouth digital OPG panoramic X-ray (`SRV-002A`, ₹1,000); external 3D CBCT referral (`EQP-010`) if roots appear close to the inferior alveolar nerve canal |
| **Chairside Duration** | 20 to 30 minutes | 45 to 75 minutes (last evening slot starts ≤ 07:00 PM) |
| **Booking Deposit (`POL-BKG-02`)** | **₹500** slot-hold deposit (100% adjusted against invoice) | **₹500** slot-hold deposit (100% adjusted against invoice) |
| **2026 Current Tariff (`DENT-019`)** | **₹1,500** per tooth | **₹6,500** per tooth |

## 3. Pre-Operative Safety & Medical Clearance Requirements

Before scheduling or commencing any extraction, patients must complete Medical & Allergy Disclosure Form `FRM-MED-01` (`DENT-025`) and sign procedure-specific Informed Consent Form `FRM-CON-01` (`DENT-028`):

1. **Blood Thinners & Cardiac Medications:** Patients taking antiplatelet or anticoagulant medications (e.g., aspirin, clopidogrel, apixaban, warfarin) must inform the dentist prior to surgery. **Never discontinue prescribed cardiac medications on your own**; our clinician will coordinate written physician clearance and prepare local haemostatic agents.
2. **Diabetes & Hypertension Control:** Blood pressure and pulse oximetry (`EQP-011`) are checked prior to surgical extraction in Operatory 2. Patients with known diabetes should eat a normal meal and take their prescribed morning medication before an extraction appointment so blood glucose remains stable.
3. **In-House Outpatient Scope:** All extractions at BrightSmile Dental Care are performed under local anaesthesia. Patients requiring intravenous sedation or inpatient general anaesthesia are referred to a tertiary hospital maxillofacial unit.

## 4. Post-Extraction Recovery & Suture Removal

* **Immediate Haemostasis:** Patients bite firmly on sterile damp gauze for 30 to 45 minutes following extraction to stabilize the blood clot in the socket.
* **Follow-Up Suture Removal:** If non-resorbable sutures are placed during `SRV-007A`, a complimentary 10-minute suture removal and healing check is scheduled **7 days** post-surgery.
* **Mandatory Aftercare & Red Flags:** Complete instructions on preventing dry socket (alveolar osteitis), diet, and urgent escalation thresholds—including calling `+91-20-5550-0199` if active bright-red bleeding persists beyond **45 minutes** (`ESC-02`)—are detailed in **Tooth Extraction Aftercare Guide (`DENT-032`)**.
