# Dental Crowns & Fixed Prosthodontic Restorations Guide (`SRV-006` & `SRV-006A`)

## 1. Clinical Purpose and Indications for Dental Crowns

A dental crown (cap) is a custom-fabricated, full-coverage fixed restoration cemented permanently over a prepared natural tooth to restore its strength, anatomical shape, chewing efficiency, and aesthetics. At BrightSmile Dental Care, crowns are fabricated in partnership with ISO-certified dental CAD/CAM laboratories using digital 3D intraoral optical scans captured on our **iTero Element 5D scanner (`EQP-012`)** in Operatory 3 (`OP-03`), eliminating messy conventional putty impressions for most patients.

### Primary Clinical Indications
* **Post-Root Canal Protection:** Protecting posterior premolars and molars (`DENT-014`) that become brittle and prone to vertical root fracture after endodontic therapy.
* **Extensive Structural Breakdown:** Restoring teeth with fractured cusps or large failing restorations where more than 50% of the coronal tooth structure is lost (`DENT-013`).
* **Cracked Tooth Syndrome:** Splinting and stabilizing teeth exhibiting incomplete enamel-dentin cracks under occlusal load.

## 2. Crown Material Options: `SRV-006` (PFM) vs. `SRV-006A` (Monolithic Zirconia)

BrightSmile Dental Care offers two clinically proven crown systems, selectable based on tooth location (anterior aesthetic zone vs. posterior molar load zone), bite forces, and budget:

| Clinical & Commercial Feature | `SRV-006`: Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal (PFM) Crown | `SRV-006A`: Monolithic Zirconia Crown |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core & Outer Composition** | Medical-grade cobalt-chromium alloy substructure layered with feldspathic porcelain | 100% solid CAD/CAM milled yttria-stabilized tetragonal zirconia (metal-free) |
| **Biocompatibility & Gum Aesthetics** | Good strength; thin metal collar may occasionally show a faint grey line if gums recede over time | Superior tissue biocompatibility; completely metal-free with natural translucency and zero dark margin |
| **Fracture & Chip Resistance** | Outer porcelain layer can occasionally chip under heavy molar bruxism | Extremely high flexural strength (> 1,000 MPa); monolithic design resists chipping |
| **Tooth Reduction Required** | ~1.5 mm to 2.0 mm circumferential reduction to accommodate metal + porcelain layers | Conservative ~1.0 mm to 1.2 mm reduction, preserving more natural dentin |
| **Laboratory Warranty** | 5-year standard dental laboratory structural warranty | **10-year dental laboratory structural warranty** (certificate issued at cementation) |
| **2026 Current Tariff (`DENT-019`)** | **₹4,500** per unit | **₹9,500** per unit |
| **Package Inclusion (`DENT-023`)** | Not included in `PKG-002` | Included in `PKG-002` (Complete Molar RCT + Zirconia Crown Bundle at ₹14,800) |

## 3. Two-Visit Crown Fabrication Workflow

Crown placement is typically completed across **two appointments spaced 5 to 7 working days apart**:

1. **Visit 1 — Tooth Preparation, Digital Scan & Temporary Crown (45–60 minutes):**
   * Local anaesthesia is administered if the tooth is vital (non-root-canal-treated).
   * The clinician shapes the tooth uniformly by 1.0–1.5 mm to create a smooth chamfer margin.
   * A high-precision 3D optical scan is recorded using the **iTero Element 5D scanner (`EQP-012`)** and transmitted electronically to the dental laboratory.
   * A chairside tooth-coloured **temporary acrylic crown** is cemented with provisional cement to protect the prepared tooth and prevent opposing teeth from drifting while the permanent crown is milled.
2. **Visit 2 — Try-In, Occlusal Verification & Permanent Cementation (30–40 minutes):**
   * The temporary crown is removed and the prepared tooth is cleaned.
   * The permanent `SRV-006` or `SRV-006A` crown is tried in to verify marginal seal, interproximal floss contact, shade match, and bite alignment.
   * Once verified, the crown is bonded permanently using dual-cure resin-modified glass ionomer or self-adhesive resin luting cement.

## 4. Temporary Crown Precautions & Lab Cancellation Policy (`REF-04`)

* **While Wearing a Temporary Crown:** Avoid sticky foods (chewing gum, caramel, chikki) and hard nuts on the temporary crown side, and pull dental floss out sideways rather than snapping it upward so the temporary cap is not dislodged. If a temporary crown loosens before Visit 2, call `+91-20-5550-0140` so reception can arrange a quick 10-minute re-cementation.
* **Custom Lab Fabrication Non-Refundable Rule (`REF-04`, `DENT-024`):** Because every crown is custom-milled to a patient's individual 3D tooth scan, once digital impressions are transmitted to the external dental laboratory after Visit 1, **50% of the crown fee** (₹2,250 for `SRV-006` or ₹4,750 for `SRV-006A`) is non-refundable if the patient cancels before final cementation.
