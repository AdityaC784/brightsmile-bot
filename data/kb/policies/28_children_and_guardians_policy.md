# Children, Adolescent Minors, and Legal Guardian Policy (`POL-PED-01`)

## 1. Legal Definition of a Minor and Clinical Governance

Under Indian law (the Indian Majority Act, 1875), any patient under **18 years of age** is legally a minor and cannot independently enter into a healthcare contract or execute clinical informed consent. Policy **`POL-PED-01`** governs accompaniment, identity verification, parental/guardian consent, and child-friendly clinical care at BrightSmile Dental Care.

Paediatric dental services are led by Consultant Paediatric Dentist **Dr. Sneha Nair, BDS, MDS (Paediatric & Preventive Dentistry, Reg. No. MH-DCI-13509, `STF-004`)** in Operatory 4 (`OP-04` — Paediatric & Preventive Hygiene Suite) on **Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays (full day), and Saturdays (morning session 09:30 AM – 01:30 PM only)**. Adolescent orthodontic care (ages 12–17) is managed by Senior Consultant Orthodontist **Dr. Meera Kulkarni (`STF-002`)** on Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays (`DENT-004`).

## 3. Verification of Parent or Legal Guardian Status

1. **Biological Parents:** Present their own valid Government Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving Licence, or Voter ID) alongside the minor's birth certificate, school ID, or Aadhaar Card confirming parental relationship.
2. **Court-Appointed Legal Guardians:** Must present a certified copy of the guardianship order or official legal documentation alongside their valid Government Photo ID before signing **`FRM-PED-02`**.
3. **Delegated Adult Escorts for Routine Orthodontic Follow-Ups (`FRM-PED-03`):** Form `FRM-PED-03` must be signed in person at the clinic by a biological parent or legal guardian during the initial orthodontic consultation, naming up to two designated adult relatives (aged 18+) authorized to escort the 14–17-year-old patient for routine bracket/aligner adjustment visits. `FRM-PED-03` **does not** authorize extractions, root canals, or changes to the approved financial treatment plan.

## 4. Child-Friendly Clinical Environment & Service Boundaries

* **Preventive Paediatric Care (`PKG-003`):** Children aged 4 to 14 years are eligible for the **Kids Cavity Shield & Hygiene Package (`PKG-003`, ₹3,600 in `DENT-023`)**, which includes two paediatric consultations with Dr. Sneha Nair (`SRV-001A`), one gentle ultrasonic scaling (`SRV-003`), full-mouth topical fluoride application, and two molar pit-and-fissure sealants.
* **Behaviour Guidance Without General Anaesthesia:** Dr. Sneha Nair uses non-pharmacological tell-show-do behaviour guidance and gentle topical/local analgesia in Operatory 4 (`OP-04`). Because BrightSmile Dental Care is an outpatient dental clinic without inpatient hospital beds (`DENT-001`), cases requiring general anaesthesia or deep intravenous sedation are referred to a tertiary paediatric hospital dental unit in Pune.
* **Cosmetic Whitening Age Restriction (`DENT-018`):** In-office LED whitening (`SRV-009`) and take-home bleaching kits (`SRV-009A`) are strictly contraindicated and unavailable for patients under 18 years of age.
