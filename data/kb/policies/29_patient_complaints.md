# Patient Complaints, Feedback, and Grievance Redressal Policy (`POL-CMP-01`)

## 1. Commitment to Fair and Timely Resolution

BrightSmile Dental Care is committed to transparent, respectful, and accountable patient care. Policy **`POL-CMP-01`** establishes a structured two-level grievance redressal mechanism with strict response Service Level Agreements (SLAs) so every administrative, billing, scheduling, or clinical quality concern is investigated objectively and resolved without prejudice to the patient's ongoing care.

---

## 2. Official Channels for Lodging a Complaint or Feedback

Patients (or parents/legal guardians of minor patients under `DENT-029`) may submit formal complaints or service feedback through any of the following channels:

1. **Dedicated Grievance & Support Email:** `support@brightsmile.example` (preferred for written audit trail and attachments)
2. **Clinic Reception & Operations Desk:** `+91-20-5550-0140` (Monday – Saturday, 09:30 AM – 08:30 PM IST)
3. **In-Person Grievance Register:** Available at the 2nd-floor reception desk, Aura Business Plaza, Baner Road, Pune 411045
4. **AI Support Assistant Handoff:** Requesting human escalation during a support chat immediately routes the conversation summary to the Level 1 Grievance Officer (`DENT-036`, `POL-ESC-01`)

| Escalation Level | Scope of Grievance | Designated Officer | Acknowledgment SLA | Final Resolution SLA |

| **Level 1: Administrative, Billing & Service Review** | Appointment delays, cancellation/rescheduling fee disputes (`POL-CAN-01`/`POL-RES-01`), refund timelines (`DENT-024`), insurance document packs (`DENT-022`), staff conduct, or general service feedback | **Vikram Gokhale (`STF-006`)** — Clinic Operations & Billing Manager (`support@brightsmile.example`) | Within **24 hours** (1 working day) with unique Ticket ID | Within **5 working days** |

*Note on Privacy Grievances: Any complaint involving personal data handling, record access delays (`POL-REC-01`), or DPDP Act 2023 rights (`POL-PRV-01`) may also be addressed directly to Data Privacy Officer Vikram Gokhale at `privacy@brightsmile.example` under the same 24-hour acknowledgment SLA.*

## 4. Investigation Workflow and Remedies

1. **Ticket Logging & Acknowledgment (Within 24 Hours):** Every complaint receives a timestamped Grievance Ticket Reference Number via email or SMS within 24 hours of receipt (excluding Sundays and the five annual public holidays listed in `DENT-002`).
2. **Objective Fact & Chart Review:**
   * For **Level 1** tickets, Vikram Gokhale audits booking timestamps, WhatsApp/SMS reminder logs (`POL-REM-01`), POS/UPI receipts, and applicable policy rules (`DENT-007` through `DENT-024`).
   * For **Level 2** clinical tickets, Dr. Aarav Deshmukh reviews pre- and post-treatment digital radiographs (`SRV-002`/`002A`), signed Informed Consent Forms (`FRM-CON-01`, `DENT-028`), and clinical chart notes, and invites the patient for a complimentary diagnostic review chairside.
3. **Written Closure & Corrective Action:** A written resolution summary is issued to the patient detailing findings and any approved corrective remedy—such as a fee waiver, duplicate-billing refund to the original payment source within 7–10 working days (`REF-06`), or complimentary clinical adjustment/remake within warranty terms (`DENT-015`).
4. **Non-Retaliation Guarantee:** Lodging a complaint never affects a patient's right to receive respectful care, clinical records (`DENT-026`), or insurance claim documentation (`DENT-022`).
